Privacy Policy

MSRX PDF Compressor  ·  Last updated: August 2026

Overview

MSRX PDF Compressor processes all PDF files entirely on your device. We do not upload, store, or transmit your documents. The app contains no networking code and makes no network requests of any kind.

Document Access

MSRX PDF Compressor accesses only the PDF files you explicitly select, whether from the app, from another app's share sheet, or from a Shortcut. All processing happens locally using Apple's Core Graphics and PDFKit frameworks. The share extension works the same way and writes nothing to shared storage.

On-Device Processing

Every compression operation occurs on-device. No PDF data is transmitted to any server at any point in the process.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect, read, or transmit the contents of your PDF files.
  • We do not require account creation or personal information.
  • We do not use your documents for training or analytics.

Analytics

We may receive anonymized crash reports via Apple's standard crash reporting to improve stability. This cannot identify you or your documents.

Children's Privacy

MSRX PDF Compressor does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. It collects no data from anyone.
