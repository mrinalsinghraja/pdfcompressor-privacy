PDFCompressor Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Overview

PDFCompressor.Shrink PDF is developed by MSRX. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information PDFCompressor collects and how it is used.

Data Collection

PDFCompressor does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or documents to external servers. All PDF processing is performed entirely on your device.

Document Access

PDFCompressor requests access to your documents solely for the purpose of compressing files you explicitly select. No documents are uploaded, shared, or stored outside your device at any time.

On-Device Processing

All compression operations are performed entirely on-device. Your PDF files never leave your iPhone or iPad, ensuring complete privacy and security.

Third-Party Services

PDFCompressor does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any data with third parties. We have no access to your documents or personal information.

Children's Privacy

PDFCompressor does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at mrinalsinghraja@gmail.com.
