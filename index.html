Your files never leave your device. Ever.

PDF Compressor v2.0 is built with privacy as its absolute foundation. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or file content — including through our new AI, batch processing, and history features. All processing happens entirely on your device.

🤖 AI Features & Privacy

Version 2.0 introduces AI-powered compression recommendations. Here is exactly how it works:

✅ AI analysis runs entirely on your device

✅ No PDF content is sent to any AI server or cloud service

✅ No internet connection is required or used

✅ Document type detection uses on-device rule-based analysis only

✅ AI recommendations are generated locally in milliseconds

⚡️ Batch Processing & Privacy

✅ All files processed locally on your device

✅ No files transmitted to any server

✅ Each file is processed independently in memory

✅ Files are never stored permanently by the app

📊 Compression History & Privacy

✅ History stored only on your device using local storage

✅ Contains only: filename, original size, compressed size, date

✅ No file content is ever stored in history

✅ Clear your history anytime from the Settings tab

✅ Never synced to any cloud or external server

📄 Information We Collect

We collect absolutely no information. PDF Compressor does not require an account, sign in, or any personal details. Files you select remain exclusively on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

🌐 Internet & Network Access

PDF Compressor does not use the internet for any functionality — including AI features. No network requests, no external servers, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.

🍪 Cookies & Tracking

We use no cookies, tracking pixels, analytics, advertising SDKs, or behavioural tracking of any kind. Your usage is completely private and anonymous.

📱 Device Permissions

PDF Compressor only accesses files you explicitly select via Apple's native file picker. No access to photos, contacts, location, microphone, or camera.

👶 Children's Privacy

We collect no data from any users including children under 13. Fully compliant with COPPA, GDPR, CCPA, and Apple App Store privacy guidelines.
